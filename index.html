About Falcon

Young company, sharp instincts, serious about the details.

Falcon started with a simple frustration: great everyday products were too hard to find, and too slow to arrive. So we built a storefront to fix both.

Today we operate a growing Amazon storefront serving customers across the USA. We source lifestyle products that are practical, well‑made and fairly priced — then lean on Amazon's fulfillment network so they reach you fast and arrive right. We're small enough to care about every order, and quick enough to keep our shelves fresh.
